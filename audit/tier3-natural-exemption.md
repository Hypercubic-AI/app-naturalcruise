# Tier-3 Natural transform exemption

Natural Cruise is exempt from the COBOL-specific tier-3 transform set. Those
relations rewrite COBOL/JCL source forms and therefore have no defined Natural
input transformation.

The applicable behavioral gates remain mandatory: exact Natural report,
work-file, diagnostic, and post-store oracles; executed ADABAS store tests;
headless batch execution; core conformance; and the interactive journey. This
record is an applicability decision, not a waiver of those gates.
